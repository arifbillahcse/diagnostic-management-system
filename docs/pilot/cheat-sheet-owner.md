# Owner — quick guide

## Every day

- **Day close**: check billed, collected, discounts, refunds and dues.
  Compare cash with the drawer.
- **Dues**: who owes money, how much, since when.
- Pending reports: anything late?

## Every week

- Discounts and refunds by staff member: anything unusual?
- **Activity log**: who changed prices, permissions or results.
- Referral report by doctor.

## Settings only you should change

- Prices and new tests (Catalog)
- Staff accounts and permissions
- Report and receipt layouts, signatures
- Modules (Settings → Modules)

## Staff leaves

Deactivate their account the same day. Their records stay.

## Your account

- Keep two-step sign-in on. Do not share your password.
- **Subscription** page: plan, licence end date, invoices.

## Problems

Write the order number, the time and what you saw, and send it to support
(WhatsApp/phone: ______________). Blockers (cannot bill, cannot print) are
fixed the same day.

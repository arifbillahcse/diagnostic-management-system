# Pilot agreement — DRAFT

> **This is a draft for a lawyer to review. It is not legal advice.** It must
> be checked against Bangladesh law (including the Digital Security / Cyber
> Security law in force, data protection rules, and health regulations)
> before it is used.

**Between:** ______________________ ("Provider"), and
______________________ ("Clinic"), address ______________________.

## 1. Purpose

The Provider lets the Clinic use the diagnostic management software ("DMS")
during a pilot so both parties can judge whether it suits the Clinic.

## 2. Period

From ________ to ________ (normally 4 to 8 weeks). Either party may end the
pilot with ____ days' written notice.

## 3. Fee

- During the pilot: ________ (free / discounted amount ________).
- After the pilot, if the Clinic continues: plan ________ at ________ per
  month / year, as agreed in writing.

## 4. What the Provider does

- Hosts DMS on a secured server with HTTPS and nightly backups kept off the
  server.
- Sets up the Clinic, imports its price list and doctors, matches layouts,
  trains staff.
- Responds to problems: blockers the same day, errors within 2 working days.
- Keeps the Clinic's data confidential and uses it only to provide DMS.

## 5. What the Clinic does

- Runs its old system alongside DMS during the parallel run (at least ____
  days) and checks the numbers daily.
- Checks reference ranges, prices and report layouts before use.
- Gives each staff member their own account and keeps passwords secret.
- Reports problems promptly with the order number.

## 6. Clinical responsibility

DMS records and prints what the Clinic's staff enter or what its analyzers
send. Medical decisions, result verification, and the correctness of reports
remain the responsibility of the Clinic and its qualified staff.

## 7. Data

- The Clinic owns its patient and business data.
- The Provider processes it only on the Clinic's behalf.
- On ending, the Provider gives the Clinic an export of its data within ____
  days on request and deletes it after ____ days, except backups that expire
  on their normal schedule.
- The Provider tells the Clinic without delay of any security incident
  affecting its data.

## 8. Limitation of liability

The Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to
________. Neither party is liable for indirect loss. *(Lawyer to set terms.)*

## 9. Software ownership

DMS remains the Provider's property. The Clinic gets a right to use it, not
to copy, resell, or modify it.

## 10. Law and disputes

Governed by the laws of Bangladesh. Disputes first by discussion, then
________.

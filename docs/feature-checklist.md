# Feature checklist against competitors

Status: working document. Fill in the "Clinic need" column during clinic
interviews and competitor demos.

**About the competitor column.** It comes from vendor websites, directory
pages, and a list supplied by the product owner. None of it has been
confirmed in a demo. Treat it as a list of claims to check, not as fact.

**Clinic need:** write Must, Nice, or Skip for each feature after talking to
clinics. A feature that competitors advertise but no clinic asks for should
not be built early.

## Questions for competitor demos

1. Which modules are in the base price, and which cost extra?
2. Is pricing per user, per branch, per module, or flat?
3. What are the setup, training, and yearly maintenance charges?
4. Is hosting included? Where is the data stored?
5. Which analyzer models are supported today, and what does each connection cost?
6. What happens when the internet goes down?
7. Can the clinic export all its data, and in what format?
8. What are the support hours and the usual response time?

## Questions for clinic owners

1. What do you use today: paper, a spreadsheet, or software? Which one?
2. What do you pay for it, including setup and yearly charges?
3. What three things would make you switch?
4. Do you have automated analyzers? Which brands and models?
5. Do you do ultrasound, X-ray, or ECG as well as pathology?
6. How many branches and how many staff use the system?
7. How do you pay referral doctors, and how do you track it now?
8. How often does your internet go down, and what do you do then?
9. Who enters data, and who only watches the numbers?

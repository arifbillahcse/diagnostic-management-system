Problems LabNest solves

Most diagnostic centers lose money and time in the same four places.

Cash that does not match

Every payment, discount and refund is recorded with who did it. The day's closing shows exactly what should be in the drawer.

Forgotten dues

Unpaid balances are listed by patient and date, and a report is held until the due is paid, if you choose.

Late or wrong reports

Results outside the normal range are flagged, a second person can verify them, and the work list shows what is still pending.

Paper and Excel

Find any patient by mobile number in seconds, with every past test and report, instead of searching registers.

Features of LabNest diagnostic software

Everything a diagnostic center does in a day, in one system.

Front desk and billing

Register patients, add tests and packages, give allowed discounts, and take payment by cash, card, bKash or Nagad. Receipts print in one click.

Lab results and verification

Sample barcodes, a work list for each section, reference ranges by age and sex, and verification before release. Corrections keep the old version.

Imaging reports

Ready templates for X-ray, ultrasound, CT and MRI that the doctor edits and signs, delivered with the patient's other reports.

Reports on your letterhead

Your logo, address, margins and doctors' signatures on every report and receipt, matched to the papers you use today.

Referral doctors

Record the referring doctor on every order and see referrals by doctor and by month.

Owner reports

Daily cash closing, income by test and by branch, discounts and refunds by staff member, and dues, from your phone.

Patient report portal

Patients open their own finished reports online with a secure code, so fewer people wait at the counter.

Branches and collection centres

Several branches under one account, with samples sent from a collection centre to the main lab and tracked box by box.

Staff and permissions

Every person has their own account. Roles decide who may give discounts, verify results or change prices.

Frequently asked questions

What is LabNest?

LabNest is online software for diagnostic centers and pathology labs in Bangladesh. It handles patient registration, billing, dues, lab results, imaging reports, report printing, referral doctors and lab analyzer connection in one system.

How much does diagnostic center software cost in Bangladesh?

LabNest is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription in three plans: Basic, Standard and Premium. The price depends on the modules and the number of branches. Contact us for the current price list; a yearly plan costs less than twelve monthly payments.

Do I need to install anything?

No. LabNest runs in a web browser on any computer, tablet or phone. You need an internet connection and a printer. Lab analyzer connection uses a small program on one computer in the lab.

Can LabNest connect to my lab analyzers?

Yes. LabNest receives results from lab analyzers that use the ASTM or HL7 standard, over a serial cable or the network, so staff do not type results by hand. We confirm your analyzer models before setup.

Who owns the clinic's data?

The clinic owns its data. Every clinic has its own separate database, and you can export your data at any time.

Is patient data safe?

Each clinic has its own database. Connections use HTTPS, backups are encrypted and tested nightly, staff sign in with their own accounts and two-step sign-in, and every change to money, results and permissions is recorded in an audit log.

Can a result or a bill be changed after it is saved?

A saved result or payment is never overwritten or deleted. A correction is saved as a new version, a refund or an adjustment, so the full history stays and the owner can see who changed what.

Can I print reports on my own letterhead?

Yes. Reports and receipts use your clinic's logo, address, letterhead margins and doctors' signatures. We match the layout to your current papers during setup.

Does it support more than one branch?

Yes. The Premium plan supports several branches and collection centres, with samples sent from a collection centre to the main laboratory and reports by branch.

How long does it take to start?

Most clinics go live within one week: we import your test list, prices and referral doctors, match your report layout, train your staff and run alongside your old system until the numbers match.

How do I pay for the subscription?

You can pay by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer. You receive an invoice for every payment from the software itself.
